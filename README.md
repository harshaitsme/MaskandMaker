## MaskandMaker

M&M is a unique advanced digital platform to buy, sell, and explore authentic Ceylon handcrafts all in one place with peace of mind.

## Features

- Browse a wide variety of authentic Ceylon handcrafts.
- Buy and sell items securely.
- Explore unique, handcrafted products from local artisans.
